Help, Camera Setup & Responsible AI

1. Best Camera Setup

  • Angle: Place your laptop or phone at a 45° to 90° side angle so knee, hip, shoulder, and ankle joints are clearly visible.
  • Framing: Keep your full body inside the frame with even lighting (avoid strong backlighting).
  • Clothing: Fitted athletic wear yields the highest landmark accuracy; very baggy clothing can obscure knee/hip landmarks.

2. Supported Exercises & Posture Checks

  • Squat: Tracks knee & hip flexion depth ("Go lower"), torso lean ("Back not straight"), and knee-over-toe tracking ("Knees past toes").
  • Push-up: Tracks elbow flexion depth ("Lower your chest more") and hip-shoulder-ankle alignment ("Hips sagging" / "Hips too high").
  • Lunge: Tracks front/back knee flexion ("Drop back knee lower"), upright torso ("Keep torso upright"), and front knee alignment ("Front knee past toes").

3. Understanding "Not Sure" States

When key joints have visibility below 60% (due to occlusion, low light, or stepping out of frame), FormCoach pauses counting and displays a Not Sure warning instead of guessing.

4. Privacy & Medical Disclaimer

  • Privacy by Design: MediaPipe Pose Landmarker and the ONNX form classifier execute 100% inside your browser (WebAssembly / WebGL). Zero frames are uploaded or saved.
  • Not Medical Advice: This tool is an academic CV prototype for MAIB AI 221 (Sports & Wellness). It is not medical or physiotherapy advice. Stop exercising if you experience pain.
